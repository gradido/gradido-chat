# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-10

The first release: a native desktop client for the chat of a Gradido
community, for Linux and Windows.

- Contacts, favourites and groups with unread marks; direct and group
  conversations in tabs.
- Sending, answering, editing, forwarding and copying messages; "also by
  e-mail" and group announcements; search in a conversation.
- Pictures and file links others sent; transfers and thank-you greetings in
  the thread.
- Groups: create, members, moderators, rename, leave; mute.
- Tray icon, sound and system notification on a new message.
- Settings: light, dark or the system's scheme; hardware acceleration, off by
  default.
- Ten languages.
- Signs in with a token copied from the browser, kept in the system's keyring.
- Works with Gradido v3.1.0 without answering a particular message, which
  that release does not have yet.

Not yet: sending pictures, starting video calls, signing in with e-mail and
password.
